Principles

🛡️

Never delete silently

Rows are dropped only if they are exact duplicates or physically impossible. Questionable rows are kept with a flag column.

📐

Standardize once

Types, ISO timestamps, 5-digit zip codes, one spelling per city, fixed column typos.

✅

Prove it

Primary and foreign keys, formats, state codes and geo coverage are checked on every run.

Rows: raw vs clean

Geolocation drops ~280k exact duplicates and 42 points outside Brazil. Problems in business tables, such as 23 orders delivered before carrier pickup or 775 orders without items, are flagged, not deleted.

Biggest fixes

🏙️

City names

Free-typed seller cities (sbc/sp, lages - sc, a zip code, an e-mail) are standardized or replaced with the zip code's city.

📍

Coordinates

~38 points per zip are reduced to one median point; 162 missing zips are filled from their 3-digit region.

🚩

Order flags

Impossible dates or orders without items are flagged. is_valid_delivered marks 96,447 trustworthy orders.

📦

Products

610 missing categories → unknown, 6 missing weights → category median, the lenght typo fixed.
